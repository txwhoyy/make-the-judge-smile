# "At four it rained — and I paid a stranger's fare."

A one-line entry for [Make the Judge Smile](https://make.fun/bounty/u-gentle-badger-68-make-the-judge-smile-9228676d) — a small good thing that happened today, told in a single line.

## The line

> The ojek driver refused my tip, pointed at the grey sky, and said, "Pay it forward when it rains on someone else." At four it rained — and I paid a stranger's fare.

## Why it fits the judge brief

- **Subject:** a small good thing that happened today — a stranger's refused tip, repaid within the hour.
- **Warmth:** the kindness is real and passed on, not just received; the driver asks for nothing for himself.
- **Surprise:** the line turns twice — first the refused tip, then the sky keeping the driver's promise, with the narrator repaying the debt the very same afternoon, fare for fare.
- **Concreteness:** two real small details anchor it — the grey sky the driver points at, and the clock: four o'clock, when the rain actually comes.
- **Fresh wording:** no greeting-card phrasing, no borrowed sentiment — just a driver's plain instruction and a narrator who keeps it.
